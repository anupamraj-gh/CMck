PROJECT SUMMARY
Project Name
Premium Interactive Food Ordering Website with WhatsApp Checkout

Overview
This project is a premium, modern, and interactive food ordering website designed for a curated menu of five signature food items (e.g., Cake, Protein Bars, Burgers, Cookies, etc.). Unlike traditional food delivery platforms, this website focuses on storytelling, transparency, and personalization by allowing customers to watch how each product is made before placing an order.

Each food item has its own dedicated landing page where users first watch a recipe video, then explore detailed product information, view the complete documented recipe, customize ingredients according to their preferences, and finally add the item to their cart.

The entire ordering experience is designed to be smooth, engaging, and visua slly premium while keeping the checkout process simple. Instead of integrating an online payment gateway, the website uses WhatsApp as the final checkout channel. Once the customer completes their order and enters their delivery details, the website automatically generates a professional invoice and redirects them to WhatsApp with a pre-filled order summary. The business owner receives the complete order details through the customer's WhatsApp, allowing manual payment collection and order confirmation.

The UI should be luxurious, clean, modern, and mobile-first, inspired by the reference design images that will be provided. Every page should feature smooth animations, elegant typography, premium imagery, and intuitive interactions to create a high-end user experience.

WhatsApp Checkout Flow
Instead of integrating an online payment gateway, the website should use WhatsApp for order placement.

When the customer clicks Pay Now, the following process should occur:

Generate the PDF invoice.

Create a formatted order summary containing:

Customer Name

Phone Number

Delivery Address

Delivery Date & Time

Ordered Products

Selected Customizations

Quantity

Grand Total

Order ID

Redirect the customer to WhatsApp with the pre-filled message addressed to the business owner's WhatsApp number.

The customer reviews the order and taps Send.

The business owner receives the complete order details through WhatsApp and manually confirms the order and collects payment.
